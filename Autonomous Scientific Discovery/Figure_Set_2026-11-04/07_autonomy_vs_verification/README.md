# Autonomy and verification

**Classification:** content `07_autonomy_vs_verification`

## Proposal claim
Autonomy and verification run opposite: the more autonomous the system, the less often its result is strongly verified (§2(2)).

**Verdict:** Partly supported: true from L2 to L3, not across all levels

## Analysis
Strong verification is 1% at L1, 31% at L2 and 25% at L3 (Spearman ρ = 0.37 over all papers). The sharper contrast is mechanism. Over 2024–2026, LLM-driven papers reach V3–V4 in 7.9% of cases (39/495), against 53.2% (50/94) for all other mechanisms; over the whole corpus, optimisation reaches 83%, symbolic/planning 55%, hybrid 21% and LLM-driven 8%.

## Limits
L3 = 12 papers; mechanism and era are confounded.
