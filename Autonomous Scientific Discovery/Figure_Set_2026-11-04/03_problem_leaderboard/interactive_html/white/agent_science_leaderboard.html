How to read the leaderboard

Strong (V3–V4)
V3: a physical or prospective experiment by the authors covering the core claim. V4: a machine-checked formal proof or third-party replication. As self-reported in the paper.
Computed (V2)
Computational or simulation validation, retrospective real data, or expert checking of a specific conclusion.
Benchmark (V1) · Claim (V0)
Benchmark scores, ablations, LLM-judge or expert rating of output quality; or a demonstration with no systematic evaluation.
Autonomy L0–L3
L1 task assistant, L2 closed-loop executor within a human-set goal, L3 autonomous researcher that chooses what to study.
Mechanism
The paper's single dominant decision mechanism (D1–D6), from the 3 Nov 2026 classification.
Problem
Quoted verbatim from the paper's papercard (section 最终科学问题); blank where the papercard has none.
