# Two instruments, and a third

**Classification:** content `05_two_instruments`

## Proposal claim
Announcement and benchmark score are unreliable instruments; the problems actually solved, on what evidence, are the third (§1).

**Verdict:** Supported as a corpus trend

## Analysis
Across the five eras, the preprint share goes 0%, 10%, 50%, 64% and 82%, and the share validated on a benchmark goes 3%, 5%, 40%, 65% and 73%. Meanwhile strong verification goes 67%, 93%, 36%, 23% and 9%. There are 65 benchmark or evaluation papers, 56 of them from 2026.

## Limits
Venue is not peer-review status; V is self-reported.
