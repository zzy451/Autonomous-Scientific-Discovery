# Cross-domain structure

**Classification:** content `02_cross_domain_structure`

## Proposal claim
Each paper's scientific-object classification is archived and may span several fields, under a three-level code (taxonomy; team discussion).

**Verdict:** Descriptive

## Analysis
198 of 662 papers sit in two or more fields, giving 422 field pairs. The strongest ties are Life sciences–Medicine (64), Chemistry–Materials (46), Chemistry–Medicine (24) and Materials–Life (18). Cross-field pairs grow 11, 5, 16, 98 and 292 across the eras; their LLM-driven share goes 0%, 20%, 75%, 74% and 93%.

## Limits
Two assignments lack second- or third-level codes.
