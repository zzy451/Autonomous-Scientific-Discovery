# Autonomy curtains (3D)

**Classification:** content `01_three_axes` › form `curtains_3d`

## Caption
One translucent wall per autonomy level (L1–L3; L0 has no papers). On each wall, the curve gives the share of that level's papers verified at V or stronger, from V0 to V4. The ★ point is the share with strong verification (V3–V4); a dashed line joins the ★ points across walls.
