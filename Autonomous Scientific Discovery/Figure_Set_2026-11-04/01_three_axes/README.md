# The three axes: openness × verification × autonomy

**Classification:** content `01_three_axes`

## Proposal claim
Problem openness (O1–O6), verification strength (V0–V4) and system autonomy (L0–L3) are separate axes. Once separated, autonomy and verification run opposite (proposal §2(1)–(2)).

**Verdict:** Axes separate cleanly; the opposition holds only from L2 to L3

## Analysis
Strong verification (V3–V4) by autonomy is 1% at L1 (2/179), 31% at L2 (144/471) and 25% at L3 (3/12; 95% CI 9–53%). It falls from L2 to L3, as the proposal says, but task assistants (L1) are the least verified, so over the whole corpus autonomy and verification are positively related (Spearman ρ = 0.37). On the mountain, 662 papers occupy 33 of the 120 (O, V, L) cells. The largest is O3·V2·L2 (178), and the strong cells are O4·V3·L2 (81) and O3·V3·L2 (54); O2 benchmark problems almost never reach strong verification (1/185). By field, the strong share runs from 0% (social and knowledge systems) and 6% (formal and computational) to 36% (chemistry), 40% (materials) and 44% (aerospace, marine and transport).

## Limits
L3 has only 12 papers. V is self-reported in the papers; L0–L3 is operationalised in RUBRIC.md and awaits confirmation against Wei et al.
