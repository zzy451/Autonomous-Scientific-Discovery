## Caption
One plate per era, stacked in time; on each, a grid of autonomy L0–L3 against verification V0–V4. Each dot is a paper at its coordinate. Colour = decision mechanism (LLM-driven, optimisation, hybrid, symbolic/planning). Brightness = verification strength: V3–V4 strong (physical experiment, formal proof or third-party replication), then V2, V1, V0.
