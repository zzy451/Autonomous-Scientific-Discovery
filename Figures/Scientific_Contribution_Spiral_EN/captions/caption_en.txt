Figure | A temporal spiral of scientific contribution across domains (simulated example). The continuous spiral progresses outward through five historical periods: up to 1997, 1998–2012, 2013–2019, 2020–2023, and 2024–2026. Each turn comprises 11 fixed, equal-angle domain sectors containing 48 second-level categories in total. Domain names appear along the outer perimeter, and category names are arranged radially within the central opening. Each category retains the same angular position and color across all five periods. Bar height, measured from the local spiral baseline on a shared 0–100 scale, represents the category's mean scientific contribution score for the corresponding period; pale gray background bars mark the 100-point reference. Bar widths are allocated according to the number of categories within each domain; neither bar width nor sector area encodes publication count or contribution magnitude. Each turn represents one period, and turn spacing does not encode elapsed time. All 240 category–period means are simulated solely to demonstrate the visual encoding and layout; they are not measured paper scores or evidence of temporal trends.
