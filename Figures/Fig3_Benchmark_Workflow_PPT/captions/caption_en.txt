Scientific contribution, problem profile and reference context are scored separately. a, Composite rubric: five branches (B1-B5) and thirteen criteria with their weights; each criterion is marked on a 0-4 anchor that maps to 0, 20, 45, 70 or 100 points, the Composite Score (0-100) is the weighted sum of mapped scores, and atomic evidence checks (bias control, coverage, consensus, sensitivity) support every mark. b, Independent O/V/L profile: problem openness (O1-O6), verification strength (V0-V4) and level of autonomy (L1-L4, computational oracle to generative architect; Wei et al.); one level is recorded per axis and the profile is not added to the Composite. c, Reference Context Index (RCI): evidence records collected at a fixed as-of date feed four components weighted 30/30/20/20, combined as an observed-weight sum with missing components retained as insufficient_data or not_calculated. d, Benchmark workflow: the paper version and evidence cutoff are fixed, an evidence packet is assembled, two LLM judges score independently, a third judge adjudicates each criterion when their Composite scores differ by more than 15 points, and human experts review evidence, disputes and samples. The report records RCI, Composite Score and O/V/L Profile separately; RCI does not enter Composite aggregation.
