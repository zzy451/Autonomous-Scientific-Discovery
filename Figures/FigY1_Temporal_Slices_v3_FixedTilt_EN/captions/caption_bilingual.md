# English Caption

Figure Y+1 | Scientific contribution and external recognition across historical periods (simulated example). Five non-overlapping tilted sheets are ordered from left to right by first public disclosure: up to 1997, 1998–2012, 2013–2019, 2020–2023, and 2024–2026. Each sheet uses identical 0–100 coordinate scales, with scientific contribution score on the horizontal axis and the Reference Context Index (RCI) on the vertical axis. The two measures are reported separately; RCI does not enter the contribution score. Each point represents one simulated paper, and color identifies one of 11 primary domains. The pale background wash indicates smoothed local paper density, not an additional score. Sample sizes appear above the sheets, and the bottom arrow indicates period order rather than elapsed time. The sheets represent distinct publication cohorts, not longitudinal trajectories of individual papers. All 1,180 records are synthetic, with no fixed post-disclosure observation window or paper-age adjustment applied; the displayed patterns illustrate the design and do not establish empirical historical trends, domain differences, or relationships between contribution and recognition.
