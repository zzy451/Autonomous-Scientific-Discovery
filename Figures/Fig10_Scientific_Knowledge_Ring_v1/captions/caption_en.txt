Thematic composition of each field and records shared between fields. Each of the eleven top-level fields occupies an equal sector; outer bars show the records in each third-level theme on a common scale, with the largest theme centred and the two largest named. Chords connect fields that share records, with width proportional to the records assigned to both fields; pairs with fewer than five shared records are grey. Inset, the largest combinations of records assigned to exactly three fields. Records can belong to several fields and themes, so counts are not additive.
